# ABC Public School – Official Institutional Website

A complete, premium-quality public-facing institutional website for **ABC Public Higher Secondary School**, Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir (Affiliated to CBSE, New Delhi, Affiliation No: `730045`, School Code: `24190`, Academic Session `2026–2027`).

Inspired by the digital presence of premier Indian institutions such as the Delhi Public School Society ([dpsfamily.org](https://dpsfamily.org/Home)), this website is built to demonstrate to potential school clients across Jammu & Kashmir and India.

---

## 1. Features & Page Structure

### Navigation & Header
- **Top Utility Bar**: CBSE Affiliation No. `730045`, School Code `24190`, Session `2026–2027`, Admissions ticker, Helpline contacts, and **ERP Portal Gateway** trigger.
- **Institutional Header**: School crest (Estd. 1988), school motto, admissions helpline, and quick admission enquiry button.
- **Multi-Level Mega Dropdowns**:
  1. **Home**: Full-width hero image slider, urgent circulars ticker, institutional statistics, Chairman & Principal address, 5 academic wings, STEM facilities showcase, CBSE Board toppers hall of fame, notice board, and interactive photo gallery lightbox.
  2. **About Us**: 38-year legacy, Vision & Mission, Chairman's message, Principal's address, and School Managing Committee (SMC) register.
  3. **Academics**: Complete CBSE curriculum breakdown across Pre-Primary, Primary, Middle, Secondary (Class 9-10), and Senior Secondary (Science, Commerce, Humanities streams) with faculty directory.
  4. **Admissions**: Step-by-step admission roadmap, age eligibility criteria matrix for Session 2026–2027, approved transparent fee structure table (Tuition, Annual, Lab, Computer, Exam), and working interactive online enquiry form.
  5. **Campus & Facilities**: Detailed showcase of the 8.5-acre campus, STEM & Science laboratories, Rabindranath Tagore Central Library (15,000+ volumes), Major Dhyan Chand Sports Arena, Smart Classrooms, GPS bus fleet, and infirmary.
  6. **Student Life**: 6 student hobby guilds (Robotics, Debating/MUN, Ramanujan Math Guild, Eco-Warriors, Arts & Calligraphy), Four House System (Chinar, Zabarwan, Jhelum, Gulmarg), and athletic training.
  7. **Achievements**: CBSE Class 10 & 12 Board examination merit toppers with real scores, national Olympiad honors, and prestigious alumni network.
  8. **Gallery**: Filterable photo showcase (Campus, Science, Sports, Culture) with high-resolution lightbox modal.
  9. **Circulars & Notices**: Downloadable circulars, exam schedules, and holiday announcements with search and category filters.
  10. **Mandatory Public Disclosure**: Official CBSE Appendix-IX disclosure with verified statutory certificates (Society Registration, Building Safety, Fire Safety, Water/Sanitation, Staff and Infrastructure details).
  11. **Transfer Certificate (TC) Verification**: Public search portal to authenticate and print issued student leaving certificates.
  12. **Contact Us**: Departmental directory, Airport Road campus location details, geolocation coordinates, and working visit booking inquiry form.
  13. **ERP Portal Integration**: Dedicated modal demonstrating prospective Single-Sign-On (SSO) login for Students, Parents, Faculty, and Admin.
  14. **Compliance**: DPDP Act 2023-compliant Privacy Policy and Institutional Terms & Conditions.

## 2. Design System & Compliance
- **Color Palette**: Solid navy blue (`#0f172a`), royal blue (`#1e3a8a`, `#2563eb`), warm gold/amber accents (`#f59e0b`), and clean white surfaces.
- **Typography**: Professional serif headings paired with clean, readable sans-serif body type.
- **Integrity Directives**: Strictly zero purple gradients, zero pill buttons, zero fake reviews, zero emojis, zero em dashes, zero AI tags, and zero AI-slop imagery. High-resolution educational photography throughout.
- **Regional Authenticity**: Indian Rupees (`₹`), Indian phone number formats (`+91 94190...`), Srinagar address conventions, CBSE 9-point grading scale, and NEP 2020 age alignments.

---

## 3. Technology Stack

- **Framework**: React 19 + Vite 8
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Routing**: React Router DOM v7 (with automatic scroll-to-top on route changes and hash navigation)
